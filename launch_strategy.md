# AMFAJ Travels and Tours - Launch Strategy

## North Star

Get 10,000 targeted followers, generate 500 WhatsApp leads, and become known for affordable Hajj and Umrah travel packages from Nigeria.

## Positioning

AMFAJ Travels and Tours helps Muslims in Nigeria prepare for Hajj and Umrah with sincerity, verified information, personalized hospitality, financial integrity, and guidance aligned with the Qur'an and Sunnah upon the understanding of the pious predecessors.

## Current Campaign Focus

The first major campaign is for Umrah, with departure planned for September 29 or 30, 2026 from Lagos and Abuja, and arrival planned for October 10 or 11, 2026.

Package details are now user-provided for website and flyer direction. Future copy should use only the package details recorded in `gemini.md` and `package_direction.md` unless AMFAJ changes them.

## Brand Promise

- Honesty with promises.
- Bespoke personalized hospitality for every pilgrim.
- Total financial integrity.
- Zero hidden fees.
- Religious guidance under approved scholarly tutelage, with exact public wording reviewed before use.

## Audience

Primary audience:

- Muslims in Nigeria who love the Sunnah.
- Prospective Umrah pilgrims preparing for a spiritually careful journey.
- Families and groups that want organized support.
- Muslims who are cautious about hidden fees, weak guidance, and poor travel coordination.

Secondary audience:

- People saving toward future Hajj.
- Relatives who may sponsor parents, spouses, or family members.
- Islamic study circles, masajid communities, and Sunnah-focused online audiences.

## Growth Thesis

AMFAJ should not try to go viral by chasing comedy, controversy, or shallow trends. The strongest viral path is trust-led content that people share because it protects worship, money, family, and preparation.

The content should repeatedly answer one emotional question:

How can I perform Umrah from Nigeria with clarity, dignity, affordability, and guidance upon the Sunnah?

## Strategic Pillars

### 1. Trust Around The Package

Build confidence around the package by making inclusions, departure locations, dates, and next steps clear.

### 2. Sunnah-Guided Preparation

Teach simple, careful lessons about Umrah preparation, common mistakes, adab, packing, intention, travel discipline, and group conduct.

### 3. Financial Integrity

Position zero hidden fees and honesty with promises as a major differentiator.

### 4. Personalized Hospitality

Show that pilgrims are not treated as numbers. Emphasize guidance, care, support, and structured communication.

### 5. Lead Capture Without Pressure

Move interested people into WhatsApp early with helpful materials and consultation, not aggressive selling.

## Channel Roles

Instagram:

- Primary awareness channel.
- Best for Reels, carousel education, shareable reminders, flyer drops, and testimonials later.

Facebook:

- Community trust channel.
- Best for older prospects, family sponsors, longer captions, group sharing, and event-style announcements.

WhatsApp Business:

- Lead conversion and relationship channel.
- Best for consultation, broadcast updates, registration readiness, and nurturing.

Google Sheets:

- Source of truth for content, leads, approvals, and reporting.

Meta Ads:

- Amplification channel.
- Best used after organic content reveals the strongest hooks and lead magnets.

## 30-Day Launch Objective

By the end of 30 days:

- Establish clear Sunni travel positioning.
- Publish consistently across Instagram and Facebook.
- Drive WhatsApp opt-ins using at least one lead magnet.
- Identify top-performing content hooks.
- Build a usable lead tracker in Google Sheets.
- Prepare the audience for WhatsApp inquiry and registration readiness.

## Offer Ladder

1. Free content: Reels, carousels, reminders, and short lessons.
2. Lead magnet: Umrah readiness checklist or Sunnah-compliant preparation guide.
3. WhatsApp opt-in: Join intending pilgrim list.
4. Consultation: Speak with AMFAJ for readiness and package updates.
5. Package inquiry: Share price, dates, inclusions, and next steps according to the approved package direction.

## Key Metrics

- Targeted follower growth.
- WhatsApp link clicks.
- WhatsApp lead count.
- Cost per lead for Meta Ads.
- Reel reach and average watch time.
- Shares and saves on educational content.
- Comments expressing intent.
- Lead-to-consultation rate.

## Decision Rules

- Double down on posts that produce saves, shares, and WhatsApp clicks.
- Avoid hooks that create attention but attract unserious followers.
- Do not publish package, visa, flight, or immigration statements outside the approved package direction.
- Do not post religious claims without review.
- Every campaign asset must include one clear CTA.

## 14-Day Immediate Action Plan

Day 1-2: Finalize brand kit, WhatsApp Business profile, Google Sheet tracker, and lead magnet title.

Day 3-4: Produce first 10 posts and 5 Reels scripts.

Day 5: Review religious, package, visa, and guarantee wording.

Day 6-7: Begin posting and invite WhatsApp opt-ins.

Day 8-10: Launch small Meta Ads test using the best organic hook.

Day 11-14: Review analytics, improve hooks, and prepare second content batch.
