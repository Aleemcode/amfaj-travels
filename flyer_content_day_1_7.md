# AMFAJ Travels and Tours - Engaging Flyer Content and Captions, Days 1-7

Launch start: Thursday, May 21, 2026, In Sha Allah.

Campaign theme for Week 1: Trust, identity, and why AMFAJ exists.

Primary conversion path: Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok attention -> WhatsApp intending pilgrims list.

Primary CTA: Send "UMRAH" on WhatsApp.

Secondary CTA: Follow, save, share, comment, or request the Umrah readiness checklist.

## Copy Guardrails

- Keep the tone clear, dignified, Sunni, sincere, and conversion-focused.
- No street language, vulgarity, mockery, or exaggerated hype.
- Use only the user-provided package price, inclusions, departure locations, and dates recorded in `gemini.md` and `package_direction.md`.
- Do not add visa approval guarantees beyond listing Umrah visa as an inclusion.
- No guarantee that worship is accepted or that every mistake is impossible.
- Use AMFAJ's approved promise: honesty, personalized hospitality, financial integrity, and zero hidden fees.
- Every flyer and caption must have one clear action.

## General Design Direction

- Use clean Hajj/Umrah visual language: Ka'bah, Masjid an-Nabawi, Ihram, prayer, travel preparation, or elegant checklist layouts.
- Keep flyer text short enough for mobile screens.
- Include AMFAJ logo, WhatsApp contact/link, and Instagram/Facebook/TikTok handles where available.
- Use one strong headline, one supporting message, and one CTA.
