# AMFAJ Travels and Tours - Website UX Research and Direction

Research date: 2026-05-31.

Purpose:

Define the website UX direction for AMFAJ based on package-led conversion, trustworthy religious travel positioning, and mobile-first lead capture.

## Executive UX Direction

AMFAJ's website should feel like a clear, dignified package-inquiry site, not a generic travel agency homepage.

The strongest experience is:

1. Introduce AMFAJ Travels and Tours clearly in the hero.
2. Show the active September Umrah package immediately after the hero.
3. Make pricing, inclusions, departure locations, and travel window easy to scan.
4. Present scholar-led guidance as a universal AMFAJ standard.
5. Move serious visitors to registration/WhatsApp quickly.
6. Use educational content to answer objections, not to bury the package.

## Research Inputs

### Product and Package UX

Baymard's product-page UX research repeatedly emphasizes that price and key product details must be easy to find. Users often abandon when pricing is unclear or difficult to locate. For AMFAJ, this means the package price, inclusions, dates, and departure locations should be easy to find on `/packages/umrah` and previewed on the homepage directly after the hero.

Applied to AMFAJ:

- Do not hide `3.5m NGN` and `1750 USD`.
- Use package cards with inclusions.
- Show Lagos/Abuja and Sept 29/30 - Oct 10/11 clearly.
- Use "Start Registration" and "full package breakdown on WhatsApp" as the next step.

### Mobile Landing Page UX

Google's mobile landing page guidance emphasizes speed, clear value proposition, and repeated CTA placement. AMFAJ traffic will likely come from Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, WhatsApp Status, and ads, so most visitors will be mobile-first.

Applied to AMFAJ:

- Use a fast, lightweight homepage.
- Avoid heavy sliders, autoplay video, and oversized scripts.
- Repeat WhatsApp CTA after major sections.
- Add a sticky mobile WhatsApp button.
- Keep forms optional at first; WhatsApp is faster for conversion.

### Official Umrah Context

Nusuk is an official Saudi platform under the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah. AMFAJ should avoid making unsafe claims around visa outcomes or official authorization. Listing "Umrah visa" as an inclusion is fine because it is part of the provided package direction, but the site must not imply guaranteed approval or official Saudi status unless AMFAJ has documentation to support it.

Applied to AMFAJ:

- Do not say "guaranteed visa approval."
- Do not imply AMFAJ replaces official requirements.
- Use careful wording: "package includes Umrah visa."
- Add a FAQ explaining that visa/travel processes follow official requirements.

### Competitor Pattern Review

Common patterns across Umrah/Hajj travel sites:

- WhatsApp and call CTAs are highly visible.
- Package cards usually show price, nights/dates, location, and inclusions.
- Trust signals often include experience, support, certifications, testimonials, and agency credentials.
- Many sites are crowded and visually noisy, which AMFAJ can improve on with a calmer, more trustworthy layout.

AMFAJ advantage:

- Clear Sunni positioning.
- Scholar-led guidance as a universal package value.
- Financial integrity and zero hidden fees.
- Nigeria and Diaspora packages presented simply.

## Recommended Website Personality

The site should feel:

- Trustworthy.
- Modest.
- Premium but not luxurious for luxury's sake.
- Organized.
- Calm.
- Direct.
- Sunni and sincere.

The site should not feel:

- Like a loud ticketing portal.
- Like a generic travel agency template.
- Like a charity site.
- Like a hype-heavy sales page.
- Like a cluttered flyer pasted into a webpage.

## Homepage UX Direction

### Above The Fold

Goal:

Make visitors instantly understand AMFAJ and the next action.

Recommended content:

- Headline: "AMFAJ Travels and Tours"
- Subheadline: "Hajj and Umrah travel support from Nigeria with honesty, personalized hospitality, financial integrity, zero hidden fees, and guidance upon the Sunnah."
- Key chips:
  - Nigeria Package: 3.5m NGN.
  - Diaspora Package: 1750 USD.
  - Departure: Sept 29/30, 2026.
  - Arrival: Oct 10/11, 2026.
  - Lagos and Abuja.
- Primary CTA: Start Registration.
- Secondary CTA: Browse Packages.

UX notes:

- Package chips can appear in the hero or directly below it, but the hero must first introduce AMFAJ.
- Do not lead with a generic hero like "Your Journey Begins Here" alone.
- Use one strong image, not a carousel.

### Package Preview

Use two package cards:

Nigeria Package:

- 3.5m NGN.
- Return ticket.
- Umrah visa.
- 2 meals.
- Accommodation in Madeenah and Makkah.
- Umrah guide.
- Ziyarah.
- 5 litre Zam Zam water.

Diaspora Package:

- 1750 USD.
- Umrah visa.
- 2 meals.
- Accommodation in Madeenah and Makkah.
- Ziyarah.
- Umrah guide.
- 5 litre Zam Zam water.

Each card CTA:

- Ask for breakdown on WhatsApp.

### Universal Guidance Standard

Place this after package cards:

"AMFAJ is intentional about scholar-led guidance across its packages. For this coming Umrah, Dr. Sharafuddeen Gbadebo Raaji is planned to join as a scholar, with pilgrims under his tutelage, In Sha Allah."

UX note:

This should be framed as AMFAJ's standard of care, not as a separate package tier.

### Trust Promise

Use four compact blocks:

- Honesty with promises.
- Personalized hospitality.
- Total financial integrity.
- Zero hidden fees.

### Final CTA

End the homepage with:

"Ready to start registration for the September Umrah package?"

CTA:

Start Registration.

## Packages UX Direction

The `/packages` page should be the package category hub.

Recommended section order:

1. Packages hero.
2. Umrah package category card: active.
3. Hajj package category card: coming soon / inactive state.
4. CTA to active Umrah package.
5. FAQ preview.

Recommended section order:

4. Diaspora package card.
5. Comprehensive package breakdown.
6. Departure and arrival details.
7. Universal scholar-led guidance.
8. What to prepare before registration.

10. Start Registration CTA.

Fast facts component:

- Departure: September 29/30, 2026.
- Arrival: October 10/11, 2026.
- Departure: Lagos and Abuja.
- Nigeria: 3.5m NGN.
- Diaspora: 1750 USD.
- CTA: Start Registration.

## Mobile UX Requirements

### Sticky WhatsApp CTA

Use a sticky bottom button on mobile:

"WhatsApp AMFAJ"

When tapped, it should open a prefilled message:

"Assalamu alaykum, I want details about AMFAJ September Umrah."

### Section Scannability

Each mobile section should answer one question:

- What is this package?
- How much is it?
- What does it include?
- When is departure?
- Where is departure?
- Who is guiding the journey?
- How do I ask questions?

### Performance

Target:

- Good Core Web Vitals.
- Lightweight images.
- No heavy page sliders.
- No autoplay video.
- Responsive layout.

## Package Card UX

Each package card should include:

- Package name.
- Price.
- Best-for label.
- Inclusions.
- CTA.

Example:

Nigeria Umrah Package

3.5m NGN

Best for: Nigeria-based pilgrims departing from Lagos or Abuja.

Includes:

- Return ticket.
- Umrah visa.
- 2 meals.
- Accommodation in Madeenah and Makkah.
- Umrah guide.
- Ziyarah.
- 5 litre Zam Zam water.

CTA:

Ask for the full package breakdown.

## Trust UX

Trust should not be hidden in the About page only.

Use trust signals across the site:

- Approved package inclusions.
- Zero hidden fees.
- Scholar-led guidance standard.
- WhatsApp direct inquiry.
- Clear FAQ.
- Terms and privacy links.
- Social media links.
- Future: testimonials, past trip photos, registration/legal credentials if available.

## FAQ UX

The FAQ should be grouped by decision stage:

### Package

- What is included in the Nigeria package?
- What is included in the Diaspora package?
- What is not included?
- What does zero hidden fees mean?

### Travel

- When is departure?
- When is arrival?
- Can I depart from Lagos?
- Can I depart from Abuja?

### Guidance

- What does scholar-led guidance mean?
- Is the guidance tied to a specific package?
- Who is Dr. Sharafuddeen Gbadebo Raaji?

### Inquiry

- How do I join the intending pilgrims list?
- Can I sponsor someone?
- What message should I send on WhatsApp?

## Visual Direction

Recommended:

- AMFAJ Brand Navy `#030C35`.
- AMFAJ Brand Orange `#FE7202`.
- White and warm off-white.
- Soft neutral backgrounds.
- Realistic Haram/Madeenah imagery used respectfully.
- Premium spacing.
- Clear package cards.
- Avoid stacked flyer-style graphics.

Avoid:

- Too many gradients.
- Crowded hero banners.
- Blinking CTAs.
- Overuse of stock family images.
- Dark overlays that make text hard to read.
- Multiple competing CTAs in one viewport.

3. Nigeria and Diaspora package separation.
4. Lagos and Abuja departure clarity.
5. WhatsApp-led conversion.
6. Dignified Sunni tone.
7. Zero hidden fees promise.
