# AMFAJ Travels and Tours - Viral Campaign Plan

## Campaign Name

Umrah With Clarity

## Core Message

Prepare for Umrah with verified information, clear promises, financial integrity, personalized hospitality, and guidance upon the Sunnah.

## Viral Mechanism

The campaign should use shareable protection content: posts people send to family, study circles, and intending pilgrims because the content helps them avoid mistakes.

## Campaign Angles

### 1. Before You Pay

Educational posts that teach prospects what to verify before joining an Umrah group.

Example hooks:

- Before you pay for any Umrah package, ask these 7 questions.
- Hidden fees are not a small matter. Know what is included before you commit.
- Do not choose an Umrah group only because the flyer looks fine.

### 2. Worship First

Content that reminds the audience that Umrah is worship before tourism.

Example hooks:

- Umrah is not tourism first. It is worship first.
- A cheap package is not enough if the worship guidance is weak.
- Your preparation starts before the airport.

### 3. Family Sponsor

Content for people who may sponsor parents or relatives.

Example hooks:

- If you want to sponsor Umrah for your mother or father, plan with clarity.
- Do not send your loved ones without proper guidance and support.
- A peaceful journey begins with transparent preparation.

### 4. Zero Hidden Fees

Trust-building content around AMFAJ's approved promise.

Example hooks:

- Affordable should not mean unclear.
- Zero hidden fees is not a slogan; it is a trust commitment.
- Ask what is included before you compare package prices.

## Content Formats

- Short Reels with one strong lesson.
- Carousel checklists.
- Static trust flyers.
- WhatsApp Status reminders.
- Facebook posts with slightly longer explanations.

## Lead Magnet

Primary: Umrah Readiness Checklist.

Secondary: Sunnah-Compliant Umrah Preparation Guide.

Both should send people to WhatsApp using simple CTAs:

- Send "UMRAH" to receive the checklist.
- Join the intending pilgrims list for verified updates.

## Meta Ads Test Plan

Start with small-budget tests only after 7-10 organic posts reveal strong hooks.

Recommended tests:

- Ad 1: "Before You Pay" checklist.
- Ad 2: "Umrah is worship first" Reel.
- Ad 3: "Join the intending pilgrims list" flyer.

Primary ad objective:

- WhatsApp messages or lead form, depending on account readiness.

## Virality Guardrails

- No controversy bait.
- No mocking other travel agencies.
- No exaggerated miracles or fear-based religious pressure.
- No pricing or visa claims until verified.
- No guaranteed worship outcome beyond approved careful wording.

## Weekly Review Questions

- Which posts produced the most WhatsApp clicks?
- Which posts were saved and shared most?
- Which hooks attracted serious questions?
- Which comments reveal objections or confusion?
- Which posts should become ads?
