# AMFAJ Travels and Tours - 30-Day Content Calendar

## Calendar Rules

- Post daily on Instagram and Facebook.

- Publish at least 4 Reels weekly.

Week 3: Hospitality, process, and financial integrity.

Week 4: Lead capture, readiness, and package anticipation.
