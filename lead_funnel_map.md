# AMFAJ Travels and Tours - Lead Funnel Map

## Why This Matters

The lead funnel map turns awareness into a controlled customer journey. Without it, AMFAJ may get likes and views without knowing who is serious, what they need, or how to follow up.

The funnel ensures that every post has a purpose and every serious prospect is captured in Google Sheets.

## Funnel Stages

### 1. Awareness

Goal: Make the right audience discover AMFAJ.

Channels:

- Instagram Reels.
- Facebook posts.
- Shareable flyers.
- WhatsApp Status shares.
- Meta Ads.

Primary content:

- Umrah preparation reminders.
- Before-you-pay checklists.
- Trust and zero-hidden-fee messages.
- Worship-first educational content.

### 2. Trust

Goal: Show that AMFAJ is serious, truthful, and aligned with the Sunnah.

Trust signals:

- Verified claims only.
- Clear do-not-say rules.
- Financial integrity.
- Personalized hospitality.
- Scholarly guidance claim reviewed before publishing.

### 3. Capture

Goal: Move serious prospects from social media into WhatsApp.

CTA examples:

- Send "UMRAH" on WhatsApp.
- Join the intending pilgrim list.
- Request the Umrah readiness checklist.
- Ask to be notified when package details are released.

### 4. Qualification

Goal: Know which leads are serious and what they need.

Google Sheets lead fields:

- Date captured.
- Full name.
- Phone number.
- City/state.
- Interested service: Umrah or Hajj.
- Travel timing.
- Number of travelers.
- Sponsor or self-paying.
- Readiness status.
- Main question.
- Follow-up status.

### 5. Nurture

Goal: Keep leads warm before pricing and package release.

Nurture content:

- Weekly preparation reminders.
- Checklist delivery.
- FAQ answers.
- Package release notification.
- Consultation invitation.

### 6. Conversion Readiness

Goal: Prepare qualified leads to decide when verified pricing, inclusions, and dates are released.

Conversion materials needed later:

- Package flyer.
- Inclusion/exclusion sheet.
- Payment process.
- Terms and conditions.
- Required documents checklist.
- Verified visa/travel explanation.

## Funnel Flow

Social post or ad -> WhatsApp CTA -> WhatsApp Business conversation -> Google Sheets lead record -> lead qualification -> nurture broadcast -> consultation -> package release.

## Operating Rule

Every awareness campaign must include a lead path. If a post does not drive follow, save, share, WhatsApp action, or trust, it should be revised.
