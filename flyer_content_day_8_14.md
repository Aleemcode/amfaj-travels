# AMFAJ Travels and Tours - Package-Led Content Pack, Days 8-14

Content block: Days 8-14 of the AMFAJ launch campaign.

Strategic shift: Week 1 introduced AMFAJ and trust. This week should make the September Umrah package obvious, concrete, and easy to inquire about.

Primary conversion path: Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and WhatsApp Status attention -> WhatsApp package inquiry.

Primary CTA: Send "UMRAH" on WhatsApp.

Secondary CTA: Ask for the full package breakdown, ask about the scholar-led Umrah, share with a sponsor, or join the intending pilgrims list.

## Package Direction To Use

Nigeria Umrah Package:

- 3.5m NGN.
- Return ticket.
- Umrah visa.
- 2 meals.
- Accommodation in Madeenah and Makkah.
- Umrah guide.
- Ziyarah.
- 5 litre Zam Zam water.

Diaspora Umrah Package:

- 1750 USD.
- Umrah visa.
- 2 meals.
- Accommodation in Madeenah and Makkah.
- Ziyarah.
- Umrah guide.
- 5 litre Zam Zam water.

Travel window:

- Departure: September 29 or 30, 2026.
- Arrival: October 10 or 11, 2026.
- Departure from Lagos and Abuja.

Scholar-led direction:

- This coming Umrah is planned with Dr. Sharafuddeen Gbadebo Raaji joining as a scholar.
- Pilgrims should be positioned as being under his tutelage, In Sha Allah.

## Copy Guardrails

- Make the package obvious this week: price, inclusions, date window, departure locations, and scholar-led tutelage.
- Do not repeat Week 1 trust/introduction angles unless they directly support package conversion.
- Do not add inclusions beyond the approved package direction.
- Do not imply visa approval is guaranteed beyond listing Umrah visa as an inclusion.
- Do not imply worship acceptance is guaranteed.
- Do not imply scholarly tutelage removes every possible mistake.
- Keep AMFAJ's voice Sunni, dignified, sincere, and clear.
- Every post must lead to WhatsApp inquiry.

## Week 2 Creative Direction

This week should feel like a public package reveal, not another awareness week.

Content angles:

- The September Umrah package is here.
- Scholar-led Umrah under Dr. Sharafuddeen Gbadebo Raaji's tutelage.
- Nigeria package versus Diaspora package.
- What the 3.5m NGN package covers.
- What the 1750 USD Diaspora package covers.
- Lagos and Abuja departure clarity.
- Why Umrah guide and Ziyarah matter.
- Sponsor/family decision-making.
