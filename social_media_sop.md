# AMFAJ Travels and Tours - Social Media SOP

## Purpose

This SOP governs how AMFAJ plans, approves, publishes, and reviews social media content.

## Platforms

- Instagram.
- Facebook.
- WhatsApp Business.

## Content Approval Rule

No public-facing content may be published until it passes these checks:

- Brand tone is dignified, modest, and Sunni.
- No street language, vulgarity, foul words, or indecent statements.
- No unverified claim.
- No pricing, visa, flight, or immigration claim unless confirmed.
- No misleading guarantee.
- One clear CTA is present.
- Religious statements are reviewed before publication.

## Content Workflow

1. Add content idea to Google Sheets.
2. Draft caption, visual text, CTA, and claim notes.
3. Assign format: Reel, carousel, flyer, story, or WhatsApp Status.
4. Check against approved schema and brand rules.
5. Mark as "Ready for Review."
6. Human reviewer marks "Approved" or "Revise."
7. Approved content is scheduled or posted.
8. Published link and metrics are recorded in Google Sheets.

## Posting Frequency

Minimum:

- Instagram feed/Reels: 1 post daily.
- Facebook: 1 post daily.
- WhatsApp Status: 1-3 approved updates daily.
- Stories: 3-5 frames on active days.

## Caption Structure

1. Hook.
2. Short explanation.
3. Trust or preparation point.
4. CTA.

Example:

"Umrah is not tourism first. It is worship first.

Before choosing a package, make sure you are also preparing your knowledge, intention, patience, and documents.

AMFAJ is preparing an Umrah journey with honesty, personalized hospitality, and zero hidden fees.

Send 'UMRAH' on WhatsApp to join the intending pilgrim list."

## Review Cadence

Daily:

- Check comments and WhatsApp inquiries.
- Reply to serious questions.
- Record leads.

Weekly:

- Review reach, saves, shares, WhatsApp clicks, and leads.
- Identify top hooks.
- Decide which posts to promote with ads.

Monthly:

- Review follower growth, lead count, cost per lead, and content themes.
- Update campaign plan.

## Response Rules

- Be polite, clear, and patient.
- Do not argue publicly.
- Move serious questions to WhatsApp.
- If unsure, say the information will be verified before giving a final answer.
- Never invent pricing, dates, inclusions, or visa details.
